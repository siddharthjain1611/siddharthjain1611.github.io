Why the gate exists

An account going quiet
tells you almost nothing
on its own.

It could be a supply problem we caused, a change in the shipper’s business, or a naturally slow ordering habit. Pitching into the first one is worse than staying quiet. Toggle the incident flag and watch the route change while the inactivity number stays exactly where it is.

Where the model sits

The policy runs first
and independently.

In the intended system a language model interprets the conversation and drafts an agenda. A separate policy layer decides eligibility and permissions, and the model reads it without being able to change it. This lab implements that policy layer and nothing else.

What these checks do and do not tell you
  • Every expected route is visible in the fixtures. I wrote both the rules and their tests, which is a weaker guarantee than it looks.
  • No language model is called. Speech quality, extraction accuracy, hallucination rate, and customer outcomes are all unmeasured here.
  • Freshness and replay status arrive as flags. Production would need real feeds, durable state, and a revalidation at action time rather than at queue time.
  • Input notes are never executed, so this is not a prompt-injection benchmark and should not be read as one.
  • Business impact needs a controlled pilot measuring incremental reorders net of full intervention cost.
